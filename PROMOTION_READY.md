* **Copy & Paste Everything Below**:

```text
Hi there!

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) is a momentum oscillator developed by J. Welles Wilder Jr. in 1978. It oscillates between 0 and 100 to evaluate momentum.

1. The Calculation Steps:
   • Compute 14-period average gains vs average losses.
   • Relative Strength (RS) = Average Gain / Average Loss
   • RSI = 100 - [ 100 / (1 + RS) ]

2. Key Reference Levels:
   • RSI >= 70: Overbought momentum zone
   • RSI <= 30: Oversold momentum zone
   • RSI = 50: Trend centerline filter
```
